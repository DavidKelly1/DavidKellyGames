Project Spatial

A research and development project for volumetric video capture. View and create 3D videos while playing your favourite VR games. We currently have over 1,700 captures shared on the platform.

VR LIV Meta Store Link

My work

This was my first project at LIV and also my first time developing for VR. I was able to quickly get up to speed and designed interactions that worked well for both VR controllers and hand tracking.

Some of my most notable learnings and contributions included:

  • Using Figma files from our designer I implemented all the UX/UI menus and button interactions, including modes for Profile, Recording, Publishing and Gallery.
  • Implemented the snapping system which allowed players to easily position the video player on any walls or ceilings in their play space.
  • Created the gallery to view/delete/publish your recordings, and also to bookmark or sort your favourites.
  • Made the intro splash screen animations, implemented a survey which helped us gather early test user feedback.
  • Plenty of bug fixing, ensuring all our builds/live updates worked well.

Camera & Recording Settings

  • Created a virtual camera interaction system allowing users to grab and move our 3 camera rig setup to their desired position and angle.
  • Developed a camera settings preset save system that created modifiable JSON files for users to customize.
  • Implemented settings to toggle microphone recording, change recording duration, change camera follow behaviour and multiple FOV options.
