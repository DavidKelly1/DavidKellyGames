My work

I was a founding member for the LIV Creator Kit SDK, I worked on many different aspects of the development journey which included programming, UI/UX design, 3D modelling and communicating with high profile developers to help them troubleshoot and integrate the SDK into their games. With over 124 million videos recorded and 1.4 million monthly active users, the SDK has become a staple in the VR community.

Some of my most notable contributions and learnings include:

  • Creating the modular Scriptable Object button interaction scripts which allowed developers to easily customize and modify the camera prefab design in their projects.
  • Learning how to leverage ML/AI to boost my efficiency while still maintaining a high standard of quality.
  • Updating and maintaining multiple camera prefabs which supported developers using the most common VR packages such as XR interaction toolkit, Meta's XR SDK, and Gorilla Locomotion like rigs.
  • 3D character design, modelling, and rigging with inverse kinematics for our public sample scene and play testing scenes.
  • Prototyping, gathering user feedback and iterating on new features for the SDK, such as livestreaming to Twitch and Youtube.

Setup Wizard

I created the in-editor Setup Wizard which guides developers through integrating the SDK step by step, reducing onboarding friction and getting projects up and running faster. It also includes a project validation page which helps solve common issues during integration, users can automatically apply fixes with just a single click.

Rapid Prototyping

A valuable skill I honed during my time at LIV was rapid prototyping. As a small tech startup it was crucial to be able to test out new ideas and iterate on them quickly based on test user feedback. I also created our test scene and player character we frequently used to replicate our most popular game integration Gorilla Tag.
